CloseWithOwner is a for-sale-by-owner marketplace. Owners list their own homes; buyers and sellers are document-verified before they can message or book a showing. This policy says exactly what data the app collects, why, and what never happens to it.

• Account: your email address, the display name and phone number you enter, and your sign-in credentials (held by our authentication provider, Supabase — we never see your password).

• Verification documents: the document photos you upload for the AI document check (government ID, and an ownership record, lender letter or bank statement). These are transmitted to our AI provider for the check. The check result (status, tier label, date) is stored on your profile; that result — never your ID — is what other users see as your badge.

• Listings: the property facts, description and photos you publish, your showing availability, and your photo-rights attestation.

• Marketplace activity: chat messages, showing bookings and check-ins, deals, closing-checklist progress, hold proposals, and the settlement agent details the parties enter.

• Purchases: credit-pack purchases are processed by Apple / Google through RevenueCat. We receive purchase confirmations and your credit balance; we never see or store your card details.

• AI jobs: the text and images you submit to an AI feature (listing facts, contract text you paste, listing photos) are sent to our AI providers solely to produce the output you asked for.

• We never sell your data. We run no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs beyond Apple's and Google's own aggregate store statistics.

• We never share your verification documents with other users. A buyer sees your badge and tier label only.

• We never hold your money: earnest money, hold fees and closing funds move only through the licensed settlement agent the buyer chooses.

Other signed-in users see your public listing, your display name and your verification badge. The other party to your chat, showing or deal sees the messages and deal details you share with them. Service providers (Supabase for accounts and data, our backend host, RevenueCat for purchases, and the AI providers for jobs you start) process data only to run the app.

Your data is kept while your account exists. Deleting your account in Settings permanently deletes your account, listings, photos, chats, showings, deals and verification record from our servers. If you no longer have the app, email info@annizon.com or use the account-deletion page linked from the store listing.

CloseWithOwner is for adults buying and selling homes. It is not directed at children and accounts are for users 18 and older.

If this policy changes, the new version is posted at the policy URL with a new effective date before it applies.
