CloseWithOwner is a software marketplace that lets a home's owner list it, lets buyers and sellers verify documents, message, schedule showings, generate documents with AI tools, and track a closing. CloseWithOwner is NOT a real-estate broker or brokerage, is NOT a law firm, and is NOT a settlement agent or escrow holder. No one at CloseWithOwner negotiates on your behalf, advises you on price or terms, or holds earnest money, hold fees or closing funds. Money in a sale moves only through the licensed settlement agent the buyer chooses.

The platform charges flat software fees (AI credits). It never charges a percentage of any sale price and takes no referral fee from any professional listed in the in-app directory.

A "Verified" badge means our AI read the documents the user uploaded and the names and key figures matched. It is a document check, not a title guarantee, not a background check, and not a promise that a lender will fund. Trust the badge for what it is; the settlement agent's title search still happens before any closing.

The AI offer writer, contract review and description writer produce informational drafts and summaries. They are not legal advice, not a substitute for an attorney, and not a state contract form unless expressly labeled as one. Have an attorney review any document before you sign it. AI contract generation is enabled state by state; where it is not enabled, the app says so.

Sellers: your listing facts, photos (you must hold the rights to every photo you publish), disclosures and payoff figures are your legal responsibility. In Virginia the seller disclosure statement is required even in a for-sale-by-owner sale, and an HOA or condo resale packet is owed to the buyer where applicable. Buyers: your offer terms are yours. Both sides: keep negotiation in the app, behave lawfully, and comply with fair-housing law — no filtering or steering by race, color, religion, sex, disability, familial status or national origin.

A $100/day hold recorded in the app is an agreement between buyer and seller only. The money is payable through the settlement agent's escrow as the parties agree in writing; the app records the proposal and never collects or holds it.

AI credits are prepaid, never expire, and are charged only when an AI job succeeds. A verification check that runs to a verdict consumes its credit even if the verdict is that documents did not match; a job that fails for a system reason is not charged. Credits have no cash value and are not refundable except where the law or the store's policies require it.

Listings are published by their owners. CloseWithOwner does not guarantee that a listing is accurate, that a home is available, or that any deal will close. If a deal falls through, earnest money is handled by the settlement agent under the signed contract — never by the app.

These terms are governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of Virginia. Disputes about a home sale are between its buyer and seller; CloseWithOwner is not a party to your sale.
