CloseWithOwner — Delete Your Account & Data

Effective: November 16, 2026 · Publisher: Annizon · Contact: info@annizon.com

Everyone who uses CloseWithOwner has a real account, because the marketplace runs on verified identities: listings, chats, showings and deals all belong to an account. You can delete your account and its server records at any time, right in the app. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open CloseWithOwner and sign in.
  2. Go to More → Settings.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

This deletes, from our servers: your account itself, your profile and verification record, your listings and their photos (including the copies in photo storage), your showing availability, the conversations and messages you took part in, your showings, your deals with their closing checklists and hold proposals, and your credit-ledger history. Deals and conversations also disappear for the other party, because they were records of your shared marketplace activity. Money is never affected: the app never holds earnest money, hold fees or closing funds — those move only through the settlement agent the parties chose.

No longer have the app?

Email info@annizon.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my CloseWithOwner account". We verify the request comes from the account's email address and delete the same records, then confirm by email.

What is not deleted

Purchase records held by Apple, Google and RevenueCat are governed by their own policies; we cannot delete the store's receipt of a credit-pack purchase. Copies already exported by the other party to one of your deals (for example a settlement packet PDF they saved) are outside our servers and outside our control.
